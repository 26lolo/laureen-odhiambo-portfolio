02 / About

The way I think is part of the work.

“Ask the question before acting.”

I am a finance professional and MBA candidate at the University of San Francisco, with a growing focus on finance, business analytics, fintech, and payments.

I tend to approach problems by asking questions before jumping to solutions. I like understanding what is happening, identifying patterns in the information, and then using that context to determine what action makes sense.

At Glovo, I worked in a fast-paced, technology-driven environment where decisions often had to be made in real time. My work involved monitoring operational information around rider availability, demand and capacity, fulfillment, and conditions that could affect service. Earlier, at Cellulant, I gained exposure to finance operations and the connection between financial processes, technology, and business operations.

Now, through my MBA, I am developing that foundation through finance, business analytics, strategy, and fintech — while staying curious about emerging markets and the ways technology can expand access and opportunity.
